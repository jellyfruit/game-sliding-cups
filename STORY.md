## World and premise

The Kingdom of Cups is ruled from the Grail Throne, and the Grail crowns only one who can bring two kings together on the board: checkmate. Seven guardian beasts once kept its borders.

**The fall.** King Aurel grew old and afraid of death. He performed the forbidden Rite of Union, pouring the power of every beast into the Grail to forge one creature that would never die. The rite broke. From the Grail rose the Chimera, and the king vanished. The Chimera's roar marked the guardians with its three-headed brand, and one by one they turned on the kingdom.

**Now.** The sun no longer rises, the knights are stone, and the Throne is held by a beast. Only the Grail-keeper Elden and his last student remain.

**世界觀。** 聖杯王國由聖杯王座統治。聖杯只承認能讓兩位國王在棋盤上相遇的人，也就是「將死」。七隻守護神獸曾守護王國的邊境。

年老的奧瑞爾國王害怕死亡，舉行了禁忌的「合一儀式」，把所有神獸的力量灌進聖杯，想造出一隻永不死亡的生物。儀式失控，聖杯中升起了奇美拉，國王從此消失。奇美拉的咆哮在守護獸身上烙下三頭印記，牠們一個接一個背叛了王國。

如今太陽不再升起，騎士都成了石像，王座被怪獸佔據。王國只剩下聖杯守護者艾爾登，和他最後的學生。

**New mechanics (decided 2026-11-24)**
- **Shifting sand (whole chapter):** sand patches drawn on the table slow cups that cross them (extra friction inside the patch). Level-only: Classic physics and the golden baseline stay unchanged.
- **Stingers (from level 21):** the scorpions mark a cup with a stinger; if it is not merged within 3 shots it drops one tier. A merged stung cup loses the mark.
- **Twin Guardians (level 24):** two guardian statues with separate health, left and right. A merge blast damages only the nearer statue; both must fall. Every few shots the guardians sting a random cup.
- **Griffin cameo (level 17):** the Griffin swoops over the table at the start and drops the first cup (existing griffin sprite).
